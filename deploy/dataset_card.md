# FIRMS Harmonizer runtime data

Runtime files loaded by the FIRMS Harmonizer app at start-up. They are generated by the
project's offline pipeline (`python scripts/run_pipeline.py etl`) from NASA FIRMS MODIS and
VIIRS active-fire records and published ancillary products; they are not raw NASA products.

| Path | Content |
| --- | --- |
| `processed/fire_cube.parquet` | Daily harmonized fire-energy cube on H3 resolution 7 cells |
| `processed/staged_firms.parquet` | Accepted, deduplicated FIRMS detection centres |
| `lookup/*.parquet` | Ancillary lookups (burned area, cloud, diurnal ratios, outages) |
| `manifest.json` | Original modification times, restored by the app for cache revisions |

Sources: NASA FIRMS (MODIS C6.1 and VIIRS 375 m active fire), MCD64A1 burned area. See the
project's `SPECS.md` and `data/ETL_SPEC.md` for methods and limitations.
